Butternut Squash Velouté

A silky, comforting velvety soup mapped out step-by-step for a delightful cooking experience.

🔪

1. The Prep Phase

15 mins
  • Peel & dice 1 butternut squash
  • Peel & dice potatoes (weight = 1/2 of the squash)
  • Chop 1/2 onion finely & mince parsley (save some garnish)
🍳

2. Sauté & Sweat

10 mins

Brown the finely chopped onion in a deep saucepan with oil or butter over medium heat.

🌱 Sweat veggies: Add diced potatoes, squash, and a pinch of salt. Cook with lid on for 5-10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
🍲

3. Simmering

30 mins

Pour in 1 liter of hot vegetable broth (pro tip: warm water in the microwave and mix stock cube directly). Bring to a boil!

🔥 Reduce heat, cover, and let simmer gently for 30 minutes until vegetables are completely tender.
✨

4. Blend & Garnish

5 mins

Blend everything smooth with an immersion blender. Stir in most of the chopped fresh parsley, keeping a pinch aside for serving decoration.

🥖

Feeling Fancy? Upgrade!

Toss cubed bread with butter and toast in a hot pan or oven until crispy golden brown. Top your velvet bowl with homemade croutons!
