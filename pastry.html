Modular Pastry Tarts

Prerolled puff or shortcrust pastry meets seasonal veggie fillings, rich eggs, and melted cheese.

🥧 1. Prepare the Pastry Base

  • Lay your choice of prerolled pastry (Puff or Shortcrust) into a ceramic or glass baking tray.
  • Poke the bottom thoroughly with a fork to prevent bubbling.

🌿 Spinach & Feta

  • Wither ~600g spinach with 2 tbsp water, 1 tbsp oil, and optional garlic.
  • Remove excess liquid. Pair with Puff Pastry & feta.

🥒 Curry Courgette

  • Mandoline-slice or grate shallots and courgettes.
  • Sauté until soft with curry powder. (Optional: add cheese of choice). Pair with Puff Pastry.

🍆 Aubergine & Tomato

  • Dice aubergine, cook high heat for 5 mins.
  • Add oil, garlic, tomato paste and/or plum tomatoes, and basil. Pair with Shortcrust & feta/provolone/halloumi.

🥚 3. Bind & Assemble

  • Remove your cooked vegetable filling from the heat source.
  • Stir in 1 to 2 eggs, your optional cheese of choice, and a handful of grated parmesan.
  • Pour and spread the mixture evenly over your poked pastry base.

🔥 4. Bake & Enjoy

  • Bake in a static oven at **200°C** for **25 to 35 minutes** until the crust is deeply golden and puffed.
  • Let cool slightly before slicing. Enjoy!
