Lasagna alla Bolognese

A straightforward, comforting classic with rich ragù, smooth nutmeg béchamel, and zero pre-cooking stress.

🔪 1. The Prep Work

  • Aromatics: Finely chop 1 onion, 1 large carrot, 2 celery sticks. Peel 1 garlic clove (remove the "garlic soul" sprout). Chop a few fresh basil leaves.
  • The Meats: 400g beef mince + 200g pork mince (the pork fat is essential for flavor!).

🍅 The Ragù Path

  • Sauté garlic in extra virgin olive oil until fragrant.
  • Add onions until soft, then add carrots, celery, and a pinch of salt. Let moisture evaporate.
  • Add beef & pork mince, break up with a spatula and brown. (Optional: deglaze with white wine). Stir in optional tomato paste.
  • Pour in 700ml tomato sauce (rinse bottle with water and add that too), stock cube, and basil.
  • Simmer low & slow for 45 mins to 1 hour. Keep it slightly liquid so dry sheets absorb it!

🥛 The Béchamel Path

  • Warm 500ml milk in the microwave; season with salt, pepper, and ground nutmeg.
  • Melt 50g butter in a saucepan, remove from heat, add 50g flour all at once, and whisk into a smooth dough ball.
  • Return to heat until golden and nutty, then gradually whisk in the warm milk bit by bit to avoid lumps.
  • Cook until thick, smooth, and creamy.

🏗️ 3. The Assembly Node

  • Base: Spread a thin layer of just sauce at the bottom of your baking dish.
  • Layering Sequence: Repeat layers of lasagna sheets (no pre-cooking needed, egg pasta preferred) ➔ Ragù ➔ Béchamel ➔ Parmesan.
  • Pro Tip: Choose your style (many thin layers or 4 rich, deep layers), but make sure no corners or edges of pasta are left dry! Finish top layer with extra béchamel and parmesan.

🔥 4. Bake & Rest (Enjoy!)

  • Bake: Cook at 180°C fan oven for about 30 minutes (follow box instructions for your specific sheets).
  • Crucial Step: Let rest for 10–15 minutes before cutting so the layers set properly. Serve and enjoy!
