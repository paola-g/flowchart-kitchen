Banana Chocolate Muffins

Quick, fluffy, and sweet muffins (makes 10–12) loaded with chocolate chunks and natural banana flavor.

🍌 1. Prep Wet & Dry Ingredients

  • Dry: Mix 180g flour, 100g sugar, ½ packet baking powder (~8g), and 50g chopped chocolate.
  • Wet: Melt 80g butter. Whisk 2 eggs together with 5 tablespoons of banana syrup (or blended banana).

🥣 2. Combine & Fold

  • Pour the wet ingredients (melted butter, eggs, banana) into your dry bowl.
  • Stir gently just until incorporated (do not overmix!), folding in the chocolate pieces.

⏱️ 3. Bake (22 Minutes)

  • Spoon the batter evenly into 10–12 muffin cups or paper liners.
  • Bake in a static oven at **180°C for 22 minutes** until golden and risen.

✨ 4. Cool & Serve

  • Let cool slightly on a wire rack before serving. Enjoy your fresh homemade muffins!
