Pressure Cooker Risotto

A lightning-fast, foolproof weeknight meal (serves 4) with modular vegetable bases and optional gourmet upgrades.

🌱 1. Choose Your Base

  • Option A (Courgette): Grate courgettes directly into the pressure cooker.
  • Option B (Butternut Squash): ~400g pre-diced squash + a pinch of chili flakes.
  • Aromatics: Finely chop a small onion. Have 250g Carnaroli rice ready.

🍳 2. Soften & Toast

  • Heat olive oil in the pressure cooker and soften the finely chopped onion.
  • Add your chosen vegetable (courgette or squash/chili mix) and cook a few minutes until soft.
  • Add 250g Carnaroli rice and toast for a few minutes while stirring.

⏱️ 3. Pressure Cook (6 Minutes)

  • Heat 600ml water (kettle or microwave) and dissolve a stock cube directly in the cooker with the rice.
  • Close the pressure cooker lid, turn heat to high until pressure builds, then cook for **6 minutes**.
  • Turn off heat and release the steam completely before opening.

🧀 Classic Finish

  • Stir in generous butter, grated parmesan, and fresh or frozen chopped parsley. Serve hot!

🦐 Courgette + Prawns

  • Immediately after opening cooker (skip parmesan), stir in fresh prawns to gently poach in residual heat. Add butter & parsley.

🍄 Squash + Mushrooms

  • Separately roast chopped mushrooms on high heat for 5 mins, toss with oil, garlic, & parsley. Fold into finished squash risotto!
