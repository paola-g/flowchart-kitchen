Cauliflower & Chickpea Curry

Roasted turmeric cauliflower, protein-packed chickpeas, rich coconut curry sauce, and perfectly steamed rice.

🥦 1. Soak & Prep Spices

  • Soak cauliflower in salted water for 30 minutes, then drain.
  • Finely chop onion; mince garlic & ginger.
  • Dry Spices: 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp coriander, 1 tsp turmeric, ½ tsp chilli powder.

🍚 The Rice

  • 1 cup rice + 1.5 cups water.
  • Simmer medium-high (no lid) until bubbly & foamy.
  • Cover, cook medium-low for 12 mins. Rest 10 mins off heat, then fluff.

🔥 Roasted Cauliflower

  • Cut into florets & chop tender leaves.
  • Toss with salt & 1 tsp turmeric.
  • Roast at 200–220°C for 20 mins (toss halfway through).

🍛 2. Build the Curry Sauce

  • Cook onions in oil for ~5 mins until translucent, then add ginger-garlic paste (cook 2 mins).
  • Add dry spices and cook until fragrant *(splash 50ml water if needed to prevent burning)*.
  • Stir in tomato paste and cook until the pan is dry.

🥥 3. Simmer & Serve

  • Add roasted cauliflower and drained chickpeas; toss until coated in spices.
  • Sprinkle garam masala and pour in coconut milk. Simmer for 15–20 minutes until reduced and tender.
  • *(Optional)* Stir in lemon juice and fresh coriander. Serve with your fluffy rice!
