Guinness Cake with Cream Cheese Frosting

A decadent, moist chocolate cake infused with dark stout beer, topped with a fluffy cream cheese and whipped cream frosting.

🍺 1. Whip Base & Dry Ingredients

  • Whip 250g room-temp butter with 330g Muscovado sugar for 5 mins. Add 3 eggs one at a time.
  • Sift together 250g flour, 100g cocoa powder, and 1/2 packet baking powder.

🥣 2. Alternate Guinness & Flour

  • Add 300ml Guinness beer to the butter mixture, alternating it with the sifted flour and cocoa.
  • Mix gently until smooth and fully incorporated.

🔥 Bake the Cake

  • Pour batter into a 24cm cake pan.
  • Bake at 150°C fan (or 180°C static) for ~50 minutes.
  • Note: Cake stays naturally moist (keep in mind for toothpick test!). Cool completely.

🥛 Make Frosting

  • Blend 350g Philadelphia with 150g powdered sugar *(regular sugar works if blended together)*.
  • Separately whip 150ml heavy cream, then gently fold into the cream cheese mixture.

✨ 3. Frost & Serve

  • Spread or pipe the rich cream cheese frosting over the cooled cake.
  • Pro Tip: Can be prepared a day in advance. Slice and enjoy!
