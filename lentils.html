Pressure Cooker Green Lentil Soup

A warming, spice-bloomed bowl of comfort featuring tender lentils, aromatic veg, and a final drizzle of raw olive oil.

🧅 1. Aromatics & Bloom Spices

  • Finely chop 1 small onion, 1 carrot, and 2 celery sticks.
  • Soften the vegetables in olive oil directly in the pressure cooker.
  • Add a spoonful of tomato paste, then toss in curry powder and let it bloom briefly in the oil.

🥣 2. Coat Lentils & Add Water

  • Add your green lentils to the pot and mix thoroughly with the spiced vegetable base.
  • Pour in 2 liters of water and drop in an optional stock cube.

⏱️ 3. Pressure Cook (15 Minutes)

  • Close the pressure cooker lid, turn heat to high until pressure builds, then cook for 15 minutes.
  • Turn off the heat and release the steam completely before opening.

🌿 4. Serve & Drizzle

  • Ladle the hot, fragrant soup into bowls.
  • The Signature Touch: Drizzle generously with crude, raw extra virgin olive oil just before eating. Enjoy!
